What is NBTF?

The game Nuclear Blast Testing Facility has been around a long time (released in 2008 by the developer Ryanblaze) and has accumulated over 41 million visits with about an 88% approval rating.

Players role-play inside a secret nuclear testing base where they can research, test, and launch nukes, defend the facility from raiders, or explore as civilians.
